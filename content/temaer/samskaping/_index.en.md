Contributions and drafts on how work is organised when many organisations take
part and none is subordinate to the others – access, task management, the
suggestion flow and notifications.

This material has two possible destinations, and they should be kept apart:

- **The practice** – experience other ecosystems can reuse – is a candidate for
  [project deliverables/frameworks/methodology](https://docs.samt-bu.no/en/prosjektleveranser/rammeverk/metodikk/).
- **The instructions** – how to contribute to this site in practice – belong
  under [How to contribute](https://docs.samt-bu.no/en/hvordan-bidra/).

If the two are mixed, the theme ends up as a user manual rather than as
experience others can apply.
