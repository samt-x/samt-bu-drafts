*Where do Simpl and the Simpl-Labs test environment belong in a roadmap for Norwegian digital infrastructure? The draft is input for discussion. The goal, the tracks and the years are proposals, not adopted plans.*

The goal is in the corner. The rings show the distance to the goal: outermost what is being explored now, innermost what has been adopted. The lines out from the goal divide the roadmap into tracks, one per theme. The dots are examples of initiatives, not a complete list.

## Proposed goal

The goal has not been decided. Three levels to choose from:

| Level | Wording | Comment |
|---|---|---|
| Overall | Norway is the most digitalised country in the world by 2030 | The vision in [The Digital Norway of the Future](https://www.regjeringen.no/en/documents/the-digital-norway-of-the-future/id3054645/?ch=2). Safe, but says little about the infrastructure |
| Middle (proposal) | Connected, trust-based shared digital infrastructure that works together with Europe | Covers interoperability, trust and European cooperation without locking into one technology |
| Focused | Trust-based data sharing across sectors and countries, also as a basis for artificial intelligence (AI) | Suitable if data and AI are to be at the centre |

Other countries phrase similar goals. Nepal, for example, has the goal "Modern, dynamic, secure and interoperable digital government".

## Where Simpl belongs

- **Track:** Data sharing and data spaces.
- **Ring:** Explore. Simpl is at an early stage, and the Simpl-Labs user group holds its kick-off meeting in Brussels on 9 December 2026. Digdir has recently joined the user group.
- **Across the tracks:** Simpl-Labs is a test environment where solutions from several tracks can be tested against the European setup: identity and credentials, access and terms of use, descriptions of data, and the interplay with Norwegian sandboxes for data sharing and AI. This is shown with dashed lines in the figure.
- **Next:** Experience from the first data spaces adopting Simpl (Simpl-Live) can provide a basis for Norwegian pilots in the next ring.

The reasoning is set out in [Relevance of Simpl for Norwegian digital infrastructure](/en/innsikt/eu-relatert/data-spaces/loesninger/simpl/relevans-for-norge/).
